Questions

The things people ask first

Is this a cheat? Will anti-cheat care?

PlexusX changes display settings through the same driver controls NVIDIA, AMD and Intel ship in their own control panels. It doesn't read game memory, inject into a game process, or touch the game at all — it changes what your monitor is being sent.

It is the same setting you could change by hand in your driver software. We can tell you what PlexusX does; we can't speak for how any particular anti-cheat behaves, so if you're worried about a specific game, check that game's rules.

Windows warned me, and my antivirus flagged it. What's going on?

You'll see "Windows protected your PC" the first time you run the installer. Click More info, then Run anyway. A couple of antivirus engines may also flag it. Here's the honest reason, rather than "just turn your antivirus off".

PlexusX isn't signed with a code-signing certificate yet — those cost a few hundred a year and we're getting one. Until then Windows has no publisher name to check, so it warns about the file itself. On top of that, PlexusX does three things scanners treat as suspicious in combination: it changes Windows settings, it asks for admin to do it, and it watches which programs are running so it can spot your game starting. Those are the features. An installer that does all three and isn't signed gets flagged on pattern, not on anything it found.

Two things worth knowing: the detection rate is a couple of engines out of about seventy, all reporting a generic heuristic rather than a named threat — and once you've installed it, updates arrive inside the app and never show this warning again. It's a one-time thing.

If you'd rather not take our word for it, scan the installer yourself before running it and ask us about anything it reports.

What does "one key, one PC" mean?

A key locks to the machine it's activated on, which is what stops keys being resold or passed around. If you change your CPU or drive, or reinstall Windows, ask in Discord and we'll release the key so you can activate on the new machine. Free, and there's no limit on how often.

Do I need an NVIDIA card?

No. NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics all work, and multi-monitor setups get the setting on every display rather than just one.

Will my colours be stuck like that afterwards?

No. Close PlexusX and your display returns to how it was. The FPS tweaks are the only thing that deliberately stays applied, and every one of them saves the previous value first, so reverting puts back exactly what was there.

Will it show up in my recordings and streams?

Not currently. Digital vibrance is applied at the driver level, past the point where OBS and other capture software read the image — so your viewers see the game as it was before PlexusX touched it, while you see the boosted version.

Making the effect visible in capture is something we're working on, and it isn't finished. If that's the only reason you'd buy, use the trial first.

What happens when my licence runs out?

The app stops applying settings and your display goes back to normal. Nothing is damaged and nothing is left behind. Renew and it picks up where it left off, profiles and all.

Can I get a refund?

The four-day trial is the full app, so you can find out before paying anything. If something goes wrong after you've bought, talk to us in Discord.

Does it slow my PC down?

It sits in the tray doing nothing until a game starts or you press a hotkey. The colour change itself happens in the driver and costs no frames.
