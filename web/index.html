Questions

The things people ask first

Is this a cheat? Will anti-cheat care?

PlexusX changes display settings through the same driver controls NVIDIA, AMD and Intel ship in their own control panels. It doesn't read game memory, inject into a game process, or touch the game at all — it adjusts what your monitor is being sent. It is the same setting you could change by hand in your driver software; PlexusX just makes it quick and per-game.

What does one key, one PC mean?

A key locks to the machine it's activated on. That stops keys being resold or shared around. If you change your CPU or drive, or reinstall Windows, message us on Discord and we'll release the key so you can activate on the new machine. That's free and there's no limit on it.

Do I need an NVIDIA card?

No. NVIDIA, AMD and Intel graphics all work. If you have several monitors, the setting applies to all of them.

Does it change anything permanently?

No. Colour settings go back to where they were when you close PlexusX. The FPS tweaks are the only thing that stays applied on purpose, and every one of them has a revert that puts back the exact value that was there before.

What happens when my licence runs out?

The app stops applying settings and your display returns to normal. Nothing is damaged and nothing is left behind — renew and it picks up where it left off.

Can I get a refund?

That's what the four-day trial is for — it's the full app, so you can find out before paying anything. If something goes wrong after you've bought, talk to us on Discord.
