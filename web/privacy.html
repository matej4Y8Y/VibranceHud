Privacy

DRAFT — not legal advice. Have someone qualified check this before you take money.

What the app sends

PlexusX checks for updates and confirms your licence is still valid. Those requests include your licence key and a PC id. Nothing else about your machine is sent.

The PC id

The PC id is a shortened one-way hash of hardware identifiers. It cannot be turned back into your serial numbers, and it exists only so a key works on one machine.

What we store

Against each key we store the plan, the dates, the PC id it activated on, and a note about the purchase. If you buy through the site, the payment provider handles your payment details — we never see your card.

What we do not do

  • No tracking of what you play, when, or for how long.
  • No screenshots, screen capture, or reading of game data.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Deleting your data

Ask on Discord and we will delete your key record. That also ends the licence.
