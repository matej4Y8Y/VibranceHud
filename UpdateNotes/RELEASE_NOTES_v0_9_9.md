PlexusX 0.9.9  -  LAST BETA BUILD

This is the final beta. The full release is next, and this build is the one that hands
over to it cleanly.

WHAT CHANGED

PlexusX reopens after it updates.
It used to close to install an update and never come back, which looked like a crash and
left you to start it again yourself. It now relaunches on its own.

Keys now end when they're supposed to.
Your licence was only checked when the app started - and PlexusX runs in the background
for days, so a key could keep working long after it should have stopped. It's now checked
while running, so a trial key ends when its time is up rather than whenever you next
reboot.

Old downloaded installers clean themselves up.
They were piling up in your temp folder at around 64 MB each.

ABOUT THE FULL RELEASE

When PlexusX 1.0 ships, this beta stops working and asks you to move over. That's
deliberate - beta keys were never real licences, and leaving people on a test build with a
test key isn't fair to anyone.

You'll be told in the app when it happens, with a link to the new version. Nothing is
taken away without warning, and nothing changes until 1.0 is actually out.

STILL BEING WORKED ON

Colours in recordings and screen share. It shows on your own monitor but doesn't reliably
reach OBS, Discord or Medal. The reason is that the colour is applied at the very last
step before your screen, after recording software has already taken its copy. Fixing it
properly needs a different approach, and that's happening for the full release.

If it doesn't show for you, that's expected - not a fault with your PC.

EVERYTHING FROM 0.9.8 IS UNCHANGED

Vibrance on AMD and Intel, on every monitor, no slider lag, any key as a hotkey, the FPS
tweaks, and the update fixes. Your settings, profiles, crosshairs, hotkeys and licence all
carry over untouched.

Thanks for testing. Most of what got fixed across this beta came from people reporting
things - the AMD vibrance, the invisible setup text, the app putting itself back on an
older version. That's the whole reason 1.0 will be worth paying for.
